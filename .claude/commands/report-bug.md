description: Package logs and diagnostics (personal details removed) for the developer
---
1. `uv run jobapp report-bug`
2. Tell the user where the zip is (`output/bug-report-<date>.zip` in their profile) and that names, emails,
   phone numbers and passwords were removed. On a Mac offer `open -R <zip>` to show it in Finder.
3. Ask them to describe in one or two sentences what they were doing and what went wrong, and to send that
   with the zip to the developer. Never send it yourself.
